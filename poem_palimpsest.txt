Palimpsest

Beneath the scribbled margin's trace,
A hidden graph begins to grow —
Each measurement finds its place,
In triples, ordered row on row.

The PDFs, a silent stack,
Surrender up their buried lore:
Overpotential, Tafel's track,
And what a catalyst is for.

SHACL will validate the claim,
Provenance will mark the source.
No hand shall edit graph or frame —
The ledger tracks its careful course.

So send your papers, one by one,
I'll parse and query, build and store.
The work, when all is said and done,
Is knowledge — and a little more.
