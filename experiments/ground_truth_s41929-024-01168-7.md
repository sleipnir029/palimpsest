## Flagged / uncertain
- **Overpotential corrected to ~210 mV** (not the 200 in the PDF-read anchor): all prose parsers say
  "~210 mV"; "200 mV" appears in **no** parse as the IrOₓ datum. paddle corrupts it to "-210 mV" → 3/4 clean.
  (A literal "200 mV" does occur once in a *theoretical* DFT-volcano context — "overpotentials of 200 and
  280 mV", Suppl. Fig 43 — not this catalyst's experimental value; don't conflate them.)
- **No discrete TurnoverFrequency value exists.** TOF is reported only as a *curve* vs potential and as
  **relative** claims ("~one order of magnitude higher … factor of five higher (TOF_decay) on rutile
  vs amorphous"). The only numeric anchors are Fig 5b axis bounds (0.001→1, docling figure-OCR) — not a
  measurement. So **no TOF row**, confirming the mechanistic nature of the paper.
- **220 mV "reduction" is CITED from ref 8** (Geiger 2018), at 1 mA/cm², not this work's own datum →
  **excluded** from the rows (would otherwise mis-attribute a literature value to this paper).
