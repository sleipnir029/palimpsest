Verdict. "Cheap + loop ≥ expensive + single-shot" holds — but the winning move is model-union of two cheap models (deepseek-flash ∪ gemini-lite, one shot each → docling 100%, paddle 95% = frontier), because they miss different values (decorrelated errors). Within-model passes are weaker: modest (union-k3), two-faced (requery), or unhelpful (reason-first). parser-union is the reliable closer for the figure-only coverage gap.

Reason-first retest: was the collapse just truncation?

deepseek-flash on docling — reason-then-format @8k: 40% → reason-then-format @16k: 66% → single-shot baseline: 94%. Raising the output cap recovered most of the catastrophe (the reasoning field had been truncating the items JSON), but reason-then-format still underperforms plain extraction. CRANE's reason-before-format does not transfer to cheap extraction models; gemini-lite was unaffected (92% throughout).

Gold-thinness: precision was understated

Of 105 distinct false-positive groups, by cross-model agreement: 32 are likely REAL (≥3 independent models extract a value the gold lacks) vs 52 likely hallucinations (1 model only). The "real" FPs are dominated by measurement types the gold never scoped — so a chunk of the apparent precision gap is gold-thinness, not model error.
